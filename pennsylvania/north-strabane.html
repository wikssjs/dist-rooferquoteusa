Roofing warranties in North Strabane: what's actually covered

Homeowners in North Strabane should be clear: there are two types of roofing warranties. First, the manufacturer’s warranty covers defects in the shingle or metal panels themselves. These warranties, especially for architectural asphalt shingles, are typically prorated after a decade or so and don’t cover poor installation, missed nails, or improper flashing work. Most require you to register the materials within a window after install, or else the warranty reverts to minimal coverage and may not be transferable at resale.

The second warranty is provided by the roofing company for their workmanship. This typically covers the labor and installation problems but can be short-lived if you pick a startup firm. In this market, a so-called “25-year labor warranty” from a business less than five years old is worth less than a modest 5- or 10-year warranty from a company with a true track record. Transferability is also limited — most local contractors offer “lifetime” coverage for original owners only, or restrict the warranty if the house is sold.

Keep in mind: improper attic ventilation, layering new shingles over old ones, or failing to register the warranty can all void coverage. Always insist on the warranty certificate and document registration details at the close of the project. Don’t count on leaflets in the bid packet — if it isn’t in writing, you don’t have it.

Roofing permits in North Strabane: what you need before work starts

For a full roof replacement (including tear-off) in North Strabane, a building permit is typically required — this falls under the Pennsylvania Uniform Construction Code (UCC), based on the IRC, and is enforced by your local municipality. Minor asphalt shingle repairs, such as a small patch or sealing a leak on a single-family home, usually do not require a permit in most Washington County boroughs. Always check with your township or borough, especially if you might change the roof’s structure, deck, or ventilation.

The permit is issued by the code authority for your local government; in North Strabane, that’s your township’s building code department, not a countywide office or state-level agency (unless your municipality has opted out, in which case the PA Department of Labor & Industry takes over). Before signing a contract, verify your roofer’s HIC registration at hic.attorneygeneral.gov and check for active workers’ compensation coverage with WCAIS (wcais.pa.gov).

Every contract over $500 must be in writing under Pennsylvania’s Home Improvement Consumer Protection Act, and you have three business days to cancel without penalty. Ask for proof of both insurance and registration before permitting work on your home — these are not optional, and any reputable roofing contractor in North Strabane will provide them as a matter of course.

Material comparison for North Strabane homeowners

Timing a roof replacement in North Strabane

Washington County’s dense contractor pool along the I-79 corridor means you can usually get bids quickly, but prime weeks fill up months ahead.
